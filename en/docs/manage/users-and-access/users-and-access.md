# Users, Roles, and Access

Choreo Integration separates two audiences, each with its own access model: the members of your own organization who build and manage integrations, APIs, and AI agents, and the external users who only interact with what you've already published.

## Access control

For your organization's own members. Members, groups, and roles control what someone can see and do in the Choreo Cloud console — deploy integrations, manage environments, publish APIs, and more. See [Control access](./access-control.md).

## User Portal

For people outside your organization. The User Portal is a separate portal that exposes your integrations to external users: after you enable it, you can publish AI agents and automations to it, and users sign in through your Choreo Identity organization to chat with agents or run automations, without access to the Choreo Cloud console. See [User Portal](./user-portal/user-portal.md).
