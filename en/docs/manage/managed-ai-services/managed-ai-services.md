# Managed AI Services

Choreo Integration provides managed services for building AI-powered integrations: retrieval-augmented generation for your AI agents, and letting users talk to a deployed AI agent by voice. Choreo Cloud provisions and operates these services, so you don't manage the underlying infrastructure.

## Available services

| Service | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [Knowledge Bases](./knowledge-bases/knowledge-bases.md) | Index content from Google Drive or Amazon S3 into a vector store, and retrieve relevant chunks for RAG. |
| [Voice Agents](./voice-agents.md) | Add a voice pipeline in front of a deployed AI agent, using LiveKit with speech-to-text and text-to-speech providers. |
